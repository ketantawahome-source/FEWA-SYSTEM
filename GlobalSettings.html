Global Settings

Switches for the whole system. Changes work at once, online and at the office.

The system is CLOSED: nobody can log in or use it until you open it again.

System activity

System openOff closes the system for everyone (staff, students and online applications) and logs everyone out.
Staff loginsOff: staff cannot log in, and staff who are logged in are logged out.
Student Portal loginsOff: students cannot log in to their portal.
Online applicationsOff: the online application form says applications are closed.
Short coursesOff hides the Short Courses tab (Settings > Program Setup) and the short course choices on a course. Nothing saved is deleted: switching it on shows them again.
