  • Capital and small letters must match the Admission Number exactly as saved: for Fewa/20, FEWA-20.jpg or fewa-20.jpg will not be accepted.
  • Each photo can be up to 5MB.
  • Photos whose name is not a student's Admission Number are skipped, so no photo goes to the wrong student.
