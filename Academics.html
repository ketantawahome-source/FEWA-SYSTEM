Semester schedule
A batch is an academic year (Settings > Organisation Setup > Batches, e.g. Sep 2026 - Aug 2027); here you set the semesters inside it (SEMESTER 1, 2, 3... with their dates). A short course (3 months) has no semesters and no dates here: its students join any day and each one's 3 months run from their own Course Start Date (Student Hub). Choose the course and the batch and press Auto populate: one period, the whole course (e.g. 3 MONTHS), is made with no dates. Choose the batch to see its semesters (and the course, to see only its periods). Pick the enrolment and period, fill in the dates and Save to add a period or change one; the pencil loads a period into the form.
Closed: no invoices, credit notes or receipts can be posted in this period.
Empty accounts dates are the same as the start and end dates. Auto populate creates period schedules for every course and enrolment of the chosen batch (only the chosen course, if one is picked). Type a start date to target its year; otherwise the current year is used. Periods already in the schedule are left as they are.

Choose the batch to see its periods.
